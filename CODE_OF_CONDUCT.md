# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

JarvisMind is for people who want to give their AI systems a memory they can trust. Everyone who takes part in the
project, in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, is treated with respect and good faith, whatever their
background, experience or opinion.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming bad intent.
- Give specific, technical feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Protect privacy. Do not post other people's memories, tokens, addresses or personal details, even to illustrate a bug.
- Credit the work of others, and say when you are unsure.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, insults, or sexualised or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information about someone without their consent.
- Attempts to get access to data, accounts or systems that you are not authorised to use, including testing against
  someone else's server.
- Disruption that makes the project harder to use or review.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and any event where the
project is represented.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour through the repository's private security advisory channel, or to a maintainer you trust.
Describe what happened, when, and where. Reports are read by maintainers, and they are handled in confidence.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may ask for a change in wording, remove content, or restrict participation when a person breaks this code.
Serious or repeated violations lead to a permanent restriction. A decision can be appealed by writing to the maintainers.
